01 / THE CHALLENGE

Capturing High-Ticket Outdoor Architecture Clients

High-end landscaping projects involve substantial investments. Homeowners looking for high-ticket garden transformations and architectural living spaces do not respond to generic contractor websites.

The platform needed to communicate elite craftsmanship, showcase botanical and masonry mastery, and make requesting an on-site consultation seamless.

02 / THE STRATEGY

Cinematic Storytelling + Decision Funnels

We architected an editorial layout pairing full-width natural lighting photography with clean geometric typography.

Visual Hierarchy

Immediate immersion with an evocative headline: "Transform Outdoor Spaces Into Living Masterpieces."

Social Proof & Metrics

Integrating project volume (100+ Projects) and 10+ years experience directly into the about section.

03 / CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE

Strategic Conversion Mechanisms Deployed

Trap 1: Dual-Path Hero Action

High-intent visitors can click "Get Your Website Like This / Request Website", while discovery-focused visitors can click "View Projects".

Trap 2: Curated Portfolio Masonry

Categorized visual proof showing fire pit patios, rooftop lounges, zen gardens, and manicured hedge gardens.

Trap 3: Multi-Option Lead Inquiry Card

Clean 3-field contact card alongside direct phone and email badges to eliminate hesitation.
