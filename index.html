// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers Before We Begin

What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with service businesses of any type—including landscapers, contractors, medical and dental practices, lawyers, real estate specialists, and commercial service companies. We focus on businesses where client trust and inbound inquiries drive revenue.

Do you build e-commerce or product stores?

No. We intentionally specialize 100% in service businesses and client-acquisition platforms. We do not build complex retail e-commerce stores with hundreds of products.

What is "The W Method" / conversion architecture?

The W Method is our internal framework that structures visual hierarchy, strategic user journeys, micro-commitment estimators, and trust anchors to guide visitors into becoming booked client inquiries.

Do I own my website and code completely?

Yes, 100%. You retain full ownership of all code, assets, design files, and content with zero proprietary lock-in.

How does SEO fit into the build?

We build technical SEO directly into your codebase: semantic HTML5 tags, Schema.org JSON-LD structured data, Open Graph metadata, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and fast Core Web Vitals architecture.
